Fintech Rebranding

Building trust through visual identity redesign

Role Junior UX/UI Designer & Team Lead
Timeline ~4 months total
Team 1 designer + 2 interns
1

Problem

The company was launching a PayPal-like payment tool for brands, agencies, and influencers to manage marketing collaboration payments. Despite having a solid product, the inconsistent and unprofessional brand identity made every advertisement and social media post look illegitimate—like a scam.

This credibility crisis was actively damaging trust with potential clients in an industry where reputation is everything.

My initiative: I identified this issue within my first two months and convinced the partners to explore rebranding options, despite initial resistance.
2

Process

Research phase: Led competitive analysis of fintech and payment platforms to understand industry visual standards and trust signals.

Initial exploration: Directed a team of 2 design interns to develop 3 distinct rebranding concepts. These first proposals were rejected as "too bold" for the company, but successfully convinced partners that the current identity was hurting business.

Strategic pivot: Given one week to create a new proposal that maintained some existing brand elements while solving core credibility issues. This constraint actually led to a stronger solution.

Implementation: After approval, created comprehensive brand guidelines and asset libraries, then led the rollout across all company touchpoints.

Timeline: ~2 months from initial pitch to final approval, plus several months for complete implementation
3

Outcome

Logo System
Scalable, flat-color design with horizontal, vertical, square, and circular versions
Color Palette
Web-safe colors with defined usage percentages and complementary accent color
Typography
Reduced from 6-7 inconsistent fonts to 2 standardized typefaces
Asset Library
Icon and image libraries for consistent marketing materials
  • Approved by initially skeptical partners who later personally congratulated the team
  • Positive external feedback from clients and industry contacts
  • Notable improvement in social media and website reputation metrics
  • Established visual credibility appropriate for fintech industry
4

My Contribution

Strategic identification: Recognized the brand problem and built the business case for rebranding from scratch.

Team leadership: Directed 2 design interns throughout the entire process, managing workload and maintaining quality standards.

Stakeholder persuasion: Created narrative-driven presentations with storytelling structure to convince resistant leadership.

End-to-end design: Led research, concept development, iteration based on feedback, and final design execution.

Documentation & implementation: Created complete brand guidelines manual and asset library for company-wide rollout.

5

Lessons Learned

About stakeholder management

Good ideas aren't enough—you need compelling storytelling to get buy-in from skeptical decision-makers.

Aim higher than you think stakeholders will accept. Feedback will naturally bring you down, so starting conservative means ending up too safe.

About process

I over-polished early sketches out of fear they'd be dismissed if they looked "unfinished." Next time I'd trust the iterative process and spend less time perfecting concepts that are meant to be rough.

About myself

I can identify problems others overlook and advocate for change even when facing institutional resistance.

Rejection stings initially, but I consistently bounce back with stronger, more refined ideas.

I'm capable of leadership and persuasive communication when given time to prepare and structure my thoughts.
